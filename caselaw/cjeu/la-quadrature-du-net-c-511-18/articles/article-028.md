# Article 28 — Costs

- **Instrument**: CJEU La Quadrature du Net (C-511/18)
- **Stage**: CJEU Grand Chamber Judgment (ECLI:EU:C:2020:791) (`2020-10-06`)
- **Document Symbol**: `Joined Cases C-511/18, C-512/18 & C-520/18`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `cjeu_sec_28`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_xhtml_akn`
- **Institutional Author**: Court of Justice of the European Union (CJEU Grand Chamber) `<cjeu@curia.europa.eu>`

[229] Since these proceedings are, for the parties to the main proceedings, a step in the actions pending before the national courts, the decision on costs is a matter for those courts. Costs incurred in submitting observations to the Court, other than the costs of those parties, are not recoverable.
