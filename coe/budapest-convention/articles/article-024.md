# Article 24 — Denunciation

- **Instrument**: Budapest Cybercrime Convention
- **Stage**: Second Additional Protocol on Enhanced Co-operation (CETS 224) (`2022-05-12`)
- **Document Symbol**: `CETS No. 224`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_24`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_pdf`
- **Institutional Author**: Council of Europe Committee of Ministers `<cm@coe.int>`

1 Any Party may, at any time, denounce this Protocol by means of a notification addressed to the
Secretary General of the Council of Europe.
2 Such denunciation shall become effective on the first day of the month following the expiration of
a period of three months after the date of receipt of the notification by the Secretary General.
3 Denunciation of the Convention by a Party to this Protocol constitutes denunciation of this Protocol.
4 Information or evidence transferred prior to the effective date of denunciation shall continue to be
treated in accordance with this Protocol.
