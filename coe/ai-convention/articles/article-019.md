# Article 19 — Public consultation

- **Instrument**: CoE AI Convention (CETS 225)
- **Stage**: Adopted Framework Convention (CETS 225) + Explanatory Report (`2024-05-17`)
- **Document Symbol**: `CETS No. 225`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_19`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_pdf`
- **Institutional Author**: Council of Europe Committee of Ministers `<cm@coe.int>`

Each Party shall seek to ensure that important questions raised in relation to artificial
intelligence systems are, as appropriate, duly considered through public discussion and
multistakeholder consultation in the light of social, economic, legal, ethical, environmental and
other relevant implications.
