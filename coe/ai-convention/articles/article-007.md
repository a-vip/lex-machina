# Article 7 — Requirements regarding respect for democratic institutions and the rule of law

- **Instrument**: CoE AI Convention (CETS 225)
- **Stage**: CAI Revised Zero Draft (`2023-01-06`)
- **Document Symbol**: `CAI(2023)01`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_7`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_pdf`
- **Institutional Author**: Committee on Artificial Intelligence (CAI) `<cai@coe.int>`

Each Party shall take all necessary measures to preserve the integrity of democratic institutions and
processes and ensure the respect for the rule of law and proper administration of justice in the
context of application of an artificial intelligence system. To this end, a thorough assessment of the
