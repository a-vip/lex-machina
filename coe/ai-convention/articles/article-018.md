# Article 18 — Rights of persons with disabilities and of children

- **Instrument**: CoE AI Convention (CETS 225)
- **Stage**: Adopted Framework Convention (CETS 225) + Explanatory Report (`2024-05-17`)
- **Document Symbol**: `CETS No. 225`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_18`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_pdf`
- **Institutional Author**: Council of Europe Committee of Ministers `<cm@coe.int>`

Each Party shall, in accordance with its domestic law and applicable international obligations,
take due account of any specific needs and vulnerabilities in relation to respect for the rights of
persons with disabilities and of children.
