# Article 10 — Access to public debate and inclusive democratic processes

- **Instrument**: CoE AI Convention (CETS 225)
- **Stage**: CAI Revised Zero Draft (`2023-01-06`)
- **Document Symbol**: `CAI(2023)01`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_10`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_pdf`
- **Institutional Author**: Committee on Artificial Intelligence (CAI) `<cai@coe.int>`

Each Party shall take the necessary measures to ensure that all interested parties, groups and
individuals enjoy equal and fair access to public debate and inclusive democratic processes. In doing
so, it shall take due account of the relevant implications of technological developments and the role
of public and private entities using artificial intelligence systems that may help shape public debate.
