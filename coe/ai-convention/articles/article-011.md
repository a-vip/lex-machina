# Article 11 — Privacy and personal data protection

- **Instrument**: CoE AI Convention (CETS 225)
- **Stage**: Adopted Framework Convention (CETS 225) + Explanatory Report (`2024-05-17`)
- **Document Symbol**: `CETS No. 225`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_11`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_pdf`
- **Institutional Author**: Council of Europe Committee of Ministers `<cm@coe.int>`

Each Party shall adopt or maintain measures to ensure that, with regard to activities within the
lifecycle of artificial intelligence systems:
a privacy rights of individuals and their personal data are protected, including through
applicable domestic and international laws, standards and frameworks; and
b effective guarantees and safeguards have been put in place for individuals, in accordance
with applicable domestic and international legal obligations.
