# Article 17 — Non-discrimination

- **Instrument**: CoE AI Convention (CETS 225)
- **Stage**: Adopted Framework Convention (CETS 225) + Explanatory Report (`2024-05-17`)

- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_17`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_pdf`

The implementation of the provisions of this Convention by the Parties shall be secured without
discrimination on any ground, in accordance with their international human rights obligations.
