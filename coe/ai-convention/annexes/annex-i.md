# Annex I — Explanatory Report to the Council of Europe Framework Convention on Artificial Intelligence (CETS No. 225)

- **Instrument**: CoE AI Convention (CETS 225)
- **Stage**: Adopted Framework Convention (CETS 225) + Explanatory Report (`2024-05-17`)
- **Institutional Author**: Council of Europe Committee of Ministers `<cm@coe.int>`

1. The Explanatory Report accompanies CETS No. 225 adopted by the Committee of Ministers on 17 May 2024.
2. Paragraph 24 clarifies that the definition of 'artificial intelligence system' in Article 2 mirrors the updated OECD AI definition (november 2023) and the EU AI Act Article 3(1) definition to ensure international regulatory interoperability.
3. Paragraph 105 confirms that under Article 16(4), Parties must evaluate bans or moratoria on AI practices that pose unacceptable risks to human rights, democracy, or the rule of law.
