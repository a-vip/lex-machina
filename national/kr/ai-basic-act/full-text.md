> Entry into force on January 22, 2026 of South Korea's comprehensive AI Basic Act regulating High-Impact AI, generative AI advance disclosure/watermarking, frontier safety assessments, and extraterritorial domestic representatives.

## Enacting Terms (Articles)

### Article 1 — Purpose & Scope — Framework Act on the Development of AI and Creation of a Foundation for Trust (Act No. 20676)

Structured statutory record (promulgated Jan 21, 2025; effective Jan 22, 2026): Asia's first comprehensive statutory AI law combining pro-innovation infrastructure promotion with risk-based oversight under the Ministry of Science and ICT (MSIT) and Presidential Council on National AI Strategy.

### Article 2 — High-Impact AI Governance & Safety Obligations (Act No. 20676)

Structured statutory record (effective Jan 22, 2026): Imposes lifecycle risk management, human oversight, impact assessment, and user-explanation duties on 'High-Impact AI' deployed in critical domains (energy, drinking water, healthcare, nuclear safety, biometrics, criminal justice, credit/lending, recruitment, transportation).

### Article 3 — Generative AI Advance Notice, Synthetic Watermarking & Extraterritorial Domestic Agent

Structured statutory record (effective Jan 22, 2026; Presidential Decree No. 36053): Mandates advance disclosure when services utilize high-impact or generative AI, visible/machine-readable synthetic content labeling for deepfakes, safety evaluations for cumulative compute frontier models, and mandatory designation of a Korean domestic representative by large foreign AI operators.
