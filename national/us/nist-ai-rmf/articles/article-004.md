# Article 4 — Core Function 4 — MANAGE (MG)

- **Instrument**: US NIST AI RMF
- **Stage**: NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 (NIST AI 100-1) (`2023-01-26`)
- **Document Symbol**: `NIST AI 100-1`
- **Institutional Author**: National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) `<ai-rmf@nist.gov>`

1. MANAGE 1: AI risks based on assessments and other analytical output from the MAP and MEASURE functions are prioritized, responded to, and managed.
(a) MANAGE 2.4: Mechanisms are in place and applied to sustain the value of deployed AI systems, and to supersede, disengage, or deactivate AI systems that demonstrate performance or outcomes inconsistent with intended use.
(b) MANAGE 3.1: AI risks and benefits from third-party resources and value chain components are regularly monitored, and risk controls are applied and documented.
(c) MANAGE 4.1: Post-deployment AI system monitoring plans are implemented, including mechanisms for capturing user feedback, appeal and override workflows, and incident response.
