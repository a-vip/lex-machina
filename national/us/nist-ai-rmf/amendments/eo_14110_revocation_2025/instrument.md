## Enacting Terms (Articles)

### Article 5 — Revocation of Executive Order 14110 & Federal Deregulatory Pivot (2025-01-20)

Structured regulatory milestone record (2025-01-20): President Donald Trump formally revoked Executive Order 14110 (Oct 30, 2023) on January 20, 2025 and replaced it with 'Removing Barriers to American Leadership in Artificial Intelligence'. Federal DPA mandatory frontier-model safety disclosures were rescinded. NIST AI RMF 1.0 (NIST AI 100-1) and NIST AI 600-1 persist as voluntary enterprise governance standards alongside FTC Section 5 consumer protection enforcement and binding US state AI statutes.
