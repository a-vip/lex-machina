## Enacting Terms (Articles)

### Article 1 — Brazil AI Regulatory Framework (PL 2338/2023) — Risk-Based Architecture & SIA

Structured legislative record (approved by Brazilian Federal Senate Dec 10, 2024; under Chamber of Deputies Special Committee PL233823 review in 2025-2026; NOT YET ENACTED): Establishes EU-inspired excessive-risk prohibitions, high-risk algorithmic impact assessments, copyright remuneration/opt-out rights for AI training data, and the National System for Regulation and Governance of AI (SIA) coordinated by the ANPD.
