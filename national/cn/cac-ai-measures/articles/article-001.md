# Article 1 — Purpose, Scope, and R&D Exemption (Articles 1–2)

- **Instrument**: China CAC AI Regulations
- **Stage**: Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services (`2023-07-10`)
- **Document Symbol**: `CAC Order No. 15`
- **Institutional Author**: Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC) `<cac@cac.gov.cn>`

1. These Measures are formulated in order to promote the healthy development and standardized application of generative artificial intelligence, safeguard national security and social public interests, and protect the lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons, and other organizations.
2. These Measures apply to the use of generative artificial intelligence technology (models and related technologies with the ability to generate text, pictures, audio, video, and other content) to provide services for generating text, pictures, audio, video, and other content to the public within the territory of the People's Republic of China.
3. Where industry organizations, enterprises, educational and scientific research institutions, public cultural institutions, and relevant professional institutions research, develop, and apply generative artificial intelligence technology, and do not provide generative artificial intelligence services to the domestic public, the provisions of these Measures do not apply.
