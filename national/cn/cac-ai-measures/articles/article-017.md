# Article 17 — Classified and Graded Supervision, Security Assessment, and Algorithm Filing (Articles 16–17)

- **Instrument**: China CAC AI Regulations
- **Stage**: Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services (`2023-07-10`)
- **Document Symbol**: `CAC Order No. 15`
- **Institutional Author**: Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC) `<cac@cac.gov.cn>`

1. Relevant competent departments shall, based on the technical characteristics of generative AI and its service applications in relevant industries and fields, implement inclusive, prudent, and classified and graded supervision.
2. Providers that provide generative AI services with public opinion attributes or social mobilization capabilities shall carry out security assessments in accordance with relevant state provisions, and fulfill algorithm filing, change, and cancellation filing procedures in accordance with the Internet Information Service Algorithmic Recommendation Management Provisions.
