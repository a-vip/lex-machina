# People's Republic of China CAC Algorithmic, Deep Synthesis, and Generative AI Regulatory Trilogy

- **Stage**: Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services (`generative_ai_measures_2023`)
- **Document Symbol**: `CAC Order No. 15`
- **Date**: `2023-07-10`
- **Institutional Author**: `Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC) <cac@cac.gov.cn>`
- **Source**: https://www.cac.gov.cn/2023-07/13/c_1690898327029107.htm

> Interim Measures for the Management of Generative Artificial Intelligence Services (jointly issued by CAC, NDRC, MoE, MOST, MIIT, MPS, NRTA) governing public-facing foundation and generative AI services, training data IP and personal information compliance, accuracy/reliability duties, and tiered regulation excluding internal R&D.

## Preamble & Recitals

(1) The Interim Measures for the Management of Generative Artificial Intelligence Services (CAC Order No. 15, jointly promulgated on 10 July 2023 by CAC, NDRC, MoE, MOST, MIIT, MPS, and NRTA, effective 15 August 2023) establish comprehensive lifecycle governance for public-facing foundation and generative AI services while introducing an explicit R&D/internal-use exemption and agile 'classified and graded' supervision.

## Enacting Terms (Articles)

### Article 1 — Purpose, Scope, and R&D Exemption (Articles 1–2)

1. These Measures are formulated in order to promote the healthy development and standardized application of generative artificial intelligence, safeguard national security and social public interests, and protect the lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons, and other organizations.
2. These Measures apply to the use of generative artificial intelligence technology (models and related technologies with the ability to generate text, pictures, audio, video, and other content) to provide services for generating text, pictures, audio, video, and other content to the public within the territory of the People's Republic of China.
3. Where industry organizations, enterprises, educational and scientific research institutions, public cultural institutions, and relevant professional institutions research, develop, and apply generative artificial intelligence technology, and do not provide generative artificial intelligence services to the domestic public, the provisions of these Measures do not apply.

### Article 4 — General Principles, Non-Discrimination, and IP Protection

1. The provision and use of generative AI services shall abide by laws and administrative regulations, respect social morality and ethics, and adhere to the following provisions:
(a) uphold the Core Socialist Values, and must not generate content inciting subversion of state power, undermining national unity, or宣扬 (promoting) terrorism, extremism, ethnic hatred, violence, obscenity, or false and harmful information;
(b) take effective measures during algorithm design, training data selection, model generation and optimization, and service provision to prevent discrimination based on ethnicity, belief, country, region, gender, age, occupation, or health;
(c) respect intellectual property rights and business ethics, keep commercial secrets, and must not use algorithms, data, platforms, and other advantages to implement monopoly and unfair competition;
(d) respect the lawful rights and interests of others, and must not endanger the physical and mental health of others or infringe upon their portrait rights, reputation rights, honor rights, privacy rights, and personal information rights;
(e) based on the characteristics of service types, take effective measures to improve the transparency of generative AI services and the accuracy and reliability of generated content.

### Article 7 — Training Data Pre-Processing, IP Compliance, and Personal Data Consent

Providers of generative AI services ('providers') shall carry out pre-training, optimization training, and other training data processing activities in accordance with the law, and abide by the following provisions:
(a) use data and foundation models with legitimate sources;
(b) where intellectual property rights are involved, must not infringe upon intellectual property rights enjoyed by others in accordance with the law;
(c) where personal information is involved, obtain the consent of the individual or comply with other circumstances prescribed by laws and administrative regulations;
(d) take effective measures to improve the quality of training data and enhance the authenticity, accuracy, objectivity, and diversity of training data;
(e) comply with the relevant provisions of the Cybersecurity Law, Data Security Law, and Personal Information Protection Law, as well as the basic scientific and technological safety requirements of relevant competent departments.

### Article 12 — Synthetic Content Labeling and User Input Protection (Articles 11–12)

1. Providers shall fulfill the obligation of protecting users' input information and usage records in accordance with the law, must not collect unnecessary personal information, must not illegally retain input information and usage records that can identify users, and must not illegally provide users' input information and usage records to others.
2. Providers shall mark generated content such as pictures and videos in accordance with the Internet Information Service Deep Synthesis Management Provisions.

### Article 17 — Classified and Graded Supervision, Security Assessment, and Algorithm Filing (Articles 16–17)

1. Relevant competent departments shall, based on the technical characteristics of generative AI and its service applications in relevant industries and fields, implement inclusive, prudent, and classified and graded supervision.
2. Providers that provide generative AI services with public opinion attributes or social mobilization capabilities shall carry out security assessments in accordance with relevant state provisions, and fulfill algorithm filing, change, and cancellation filing procedures in accordance with the Internet Information Service Algorithmic Recommendation Management Provisions.
