# Recital (58)

- **Instrument**: EU GDPR
- **Stage**: European Commission Proposal (`2012-01-25`)
- **Author**: European Commission `<commission@europa.eu>`

(58) Every natural person should have the right not to be subject to a measure which is based on profiling by means of automated processing. However, such measure should be allowed when expressly authorised by law, carried out in the course of entering or performance of a contract, or when the data subject has given his consent.
