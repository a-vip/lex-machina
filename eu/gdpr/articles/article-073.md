# Article 73

- **Instrument**: EU GDPR
- **Stage**: 2018 Consolidated Official Journal Text (`2018-05-23`)
- **Document Symbol**: `OJ L 127, 23.5.2018`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_73`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_xhtml_akn`
- **Institutional Author**: Council of the European Union `<council@consilium.europa.eu>`

The Board shall elect a chair and two deputy chairs from amongst its members by simple majority.
The term of office of the Chair and of the deputy chairs shall be five years and be renewable once.
