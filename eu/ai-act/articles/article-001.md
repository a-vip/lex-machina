# Article 1 — Subject matter

- **Instrument**: EU AI Act
- **Stage**: European Parliament Negotiating Mandate (`2023-06-14`)
- **Document Symbol**: `P9_TA(2023)0236`
- **Institutional Author**: European Parliament `<parliament@europa.eu>`

1. The purpose of this Regulation is to promote the uptake of human-centric and trustworthy artificial intelligence and to ensure a high level of protection of health, safety, fundamental rights, democracy and the rule of law and the environment from harmful effects of artificial intelligence systems in the Union while supporting innovation.
2. This Regulation lays down:
(a) harmonised rules for the placing on the market, the putting into service and the use of artificial intelligence systems ('AI systems') in the Union;
(b) prohibitions of certain artificial intelligence practices;
(c) specific requirements for high-risk AI systems and obligations for operators of such systems;
(d) harmonised transparency rules for certain AI systems and foundation models;
(e) rules on market monitoring, market surveillance, governance and enforcement.
