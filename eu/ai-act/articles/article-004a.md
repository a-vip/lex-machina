# Article 4a — Compliance of general purpose AI systems with this Regulation

- **Instrument**: EU AI Act
- **Stage**: Council of the EU General Approach (`2022-12-06`)
- **Document Symbol**: `14954/22`
- **Institutional Author**: Council of the European Union `<council@consilium.europa.eu>`

1. Without prejudice to Articles 5, 52, 53 and 69 of this Regulation, general purpose AI systems shall only be subject to the requirements and obligations of this Regulation where, in accordance with this Article, they may be used as high-risk AI systems or as components of high-risk AI systems.
2. Providers of general purpose AI systems that may be used as high-risk AI systems or as components of high-risk AI systems shall comply, as from the date of application of the implementing acts adopted by the Commission within 18 months after the entry into force of this Regulation, with the requirements established in Title III, Chapter 2.
