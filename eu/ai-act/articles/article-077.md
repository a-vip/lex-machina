In Article 22(5) of Regulation (EU) No 168/2013, the following subparagraph is added:
“
When adopting delegated acts pursuant to the first subparagraph concerning
Artificial Intelligence systems which
are safety components in the meaning of
Regulation (EU) YYY/XX on [Artificial Intelligence] of the European Parliament
and of the Council*, the requirements set out in Title III, Chapter 2 of that
Regulation shall be taken into account.

gulation (EU) YYY/XX [on Artificial Intelligence] (OJ …).”
