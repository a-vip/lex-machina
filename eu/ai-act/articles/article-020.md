# Article 20 — Parliament Mandate Amendments — Article 20

- **Instrument**: EU AI Act
- **Stage**: European Parliament Negotiating Mandate (`2023-06-14`)

- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_20`

1. Providers of high-risk AI systems shall keep the logs automatically generated by their high-risk AI systems, to the extent such logs are under their control
. Without prejudice to applicable Union or national law,
the logs shall be kept for a period
of at least 6 months. The retention period shall be in accordance with industry standards and
appropriate
to
the intended purpose of high-risk AI system.
