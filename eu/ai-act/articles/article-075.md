# Article 75 — Amendment to Regulation (EC) No 300/2008

In Article 4(3) of Regulation (EC) No 300/2008, the following subparagraph is added:
“
When adopting
detailed measures related to technical specifications and procedures
for approval and use of security equipment concerning Artificial Intelligence systems
in the meaning of Regulation (EU) YYY/XX [on Artificial Intelligence] of the
European Parliament and
of the Council*, the requirements set out in Chapter 2, Title
III of that Regulation shall be taken into account.”
__________
* Regulation (EU) YYY/XX [on Artificial Intelligence] (OJ …).”“
