# Article 4b — AI literacy

- **Instrument**: EU AI Act
- **Stage**: Provisional Trilogue Agreement Text (`2024-01-26`)
- **Document Symbol**: `ST 5662/24`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_4b`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_pdf`
- **Institutional Author**: EU Trilogue Negotiators `<trilogue@europa.eu>`

Providers and deployers of AI systems shall take measures to ensure, to their best extent, a
sufficient level of AI literacy of their staff and oth
er persons dealing with the operation and
use of AI systems on their behalf, taking into account their technical knowledge,
experience, education and training and the context the AI systems are to be used in, and
considering the persons or groups of person
s on which the AI systems are to be used.
