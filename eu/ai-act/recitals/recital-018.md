# Recital (18)

- **Instrument**: EU AI Act
- **Stage**: Council of the EU General Approach (`2022-12-06`)
- **Author**: Council of the European Union `<council@consilium.europa.eu>`

(18) The use of AI systems for 'real-time' remote biometric identification of natural persons in publicly accessible spaces for the purpose of law enforcement is considered particularly intrusive in the rights and freedoms of the concerned persons. It should therefore be prohibited, except in exhaustively listed and narrowly defined situations where the use is strictly necessary to achieve a substantial public interest by law enforcement authorities or on their behalf.
