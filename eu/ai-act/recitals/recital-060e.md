# Recital (60e)

- **Instrument**: EU AI Act
- **Stage**: European Parliament Negotiating Mandate (`2023-06-14`)
- **Author**: European Parliament `<parliament@europa.eu>`

(60e) Foundation models are a new and fast-evolving development in the field of artificial intelligence. They are trained on broad data at scale, are designed for generality of output, and can be adapted to a wide range of distinctive tasks. Providers of foundation models should ensure compliance with horizontal transparency, data governance, energy efficiency, and copyright summary obligations before making them available on the market, including when distributed under open-source licences where generative foundation models are concerned.
