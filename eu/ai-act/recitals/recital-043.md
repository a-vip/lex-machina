# Recital (43)

- **Instrument**: EU AI Act
- **Stage**: European Parliament Negotiating Mandate (`2023-06-14`)
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `rct_43`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_html`
- **Author**: European Parliament `<parliament@europa.eu>`

Requirements should apply to high-risk AI systems as regards the quality
and relevance
of data sets used, technical documentation and record-keeping, transparency and the provision of information to
deployers
, human oversight, and robustness, accuracy and cybersecurity. Those requirements are necessary to effectively mitigate the risks for health, safety and fundamental rights, as
well as the environment, democracy and rule of law, as
applicable in the light of the intended purpose
or reasonably foreseeable misuse
of the system, and no other less trade restrictive measures are reasonably available, thus avoiding unjustified restrictions to trade.
