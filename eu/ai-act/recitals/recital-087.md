# Recital (87)

- **Instrument**: EU AI Act
- **Stage**: European Parliament Negotiating Mandate (`2023-06-14`)
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `rct_87`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_html`
- **Author**: European Parliament `<parliament@europa.eu>`

As reliable information on the resource and energy use, waste production and other environmental impact of AI systems and related ICT technology, including software, hardware and in particular data centres, is limited, the Commission should introduce of an adequate methodology to measure the environmental impact and effectiveness of this Regulation in light of the Union environmental and climate objectives.
