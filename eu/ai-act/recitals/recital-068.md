# Recital (68)

- **Instrument**: EU AI Act
- **Stage**: European Parliament Negotiating Mandate (`2023-06-14`)
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `rct_68`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_html`
- **Author**: European Parliament `<parliament@europa.eu>`

Under certain conditions, rapid availability of innovative technologies may be crucial for health and safety of persons
, the environment and climate change
and for society as a whole. It is thus appropriate that under exceptional reasons of protection of life and health of natural persons
, environmental protection
and the protection of
critical infrastructure
, Member States could authorise the placing on the market or putting into service of AI systems which have not undergone a conformity assessment.
