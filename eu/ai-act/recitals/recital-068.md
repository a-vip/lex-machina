(68)
Under certain conditions, rapid availability of innovative technologies may be crucial for
health and safety of persons and for society as a whole. It is thus
appropriate that under
exceptional reasons of public security or protection of life and health of natural persons and
the protection of industrial and commercial property, Member States could authorise the
placing on the market or putting into service of
AI systems which have not undergone a
conformity assessment.
