# Recital (28)

- **Instrument**: EU AI Act
- **Stage**: European Commission Proposal (`2021-04-21`)
- **Author**: European Commission `<commission@europa.eu>`

(28) AI systems could produce adverse outcomes to health and safety of persons, in particular when such systems operate as components of products. Consistently with the objectives of Union harmonisation legislation to facilitate the free movement of products in the internal market and to ensure that only safe and otherwise compliant products find their way into the market, it is important that the safety risks that may be generated by a product as a whole due to its digital components, including AI systems, are duly prevented and mitigated.
