(51)
Cybersecur
ity plays a crucial role in ensuring that AI systems are resilient against attempts
to alter their use, behaviour, performance or compromise their security properties by
malicious third parties exploiting the system’s vulnerabilities. Cyberattacks against
AI
systems can leverage AI specific assets, such as training data sets (e.g. data poisoning) or
trained models (e.g. adversarial attacks), or exploit vulnerabilities in the AI system’s digital
assets or the underlying ICT infrastructure. To ensure a level
of cybersecurity appropriate to
the risks, suitable measures should therefore be taken by the providers of high
-
risk AI
systems, also taking into account as appropriate the underlying ICT infrastructure.
