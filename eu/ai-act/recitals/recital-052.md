# Recital (52)

- **Instrument**: EU AI Act
- **Stage**: European Commission Proposal (`2021-04-21`)
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `rct_52`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_xhtml_akn`
- **Author**: European Commission `<commission@europa.eu>`

(52) As part of Union harmonisation legislation, rules applicable to the placing on the market, putting into service and use of high-risk AI systems should be laid down consistently with Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council
51
setting out the requirements for accreditation and the market surveillance of products, Decision No 768/2008/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council
52
on a common framework for the marketing of products and Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 of the European Parliament and of the Council
53
on market surveillance and compliance of products (‘ New Legislative Framework for the marketing of products’).
