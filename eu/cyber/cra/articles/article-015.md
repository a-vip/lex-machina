# Article 15 — Cases in which obligations of manufacturers apply to importers and distributors

- **Instrument**: EU Cyber Resilience Act
- **Stage**: European Commission Proposal (`2022-09-15`)
- **Document Symbol**: `COM(2022) 454 final`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_15`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_xhtml_akn`
- **Institutional Author**: European Commission `<commission@europa.eu>`

An importer or distributor shall be considered a manufacturer for the purposes of this Regulation and shall be subject to the obligations of the manufacturer set out in Articles 10 and 11(1), (2),
(4) and (7) where that importer or distributor places a product with digital elements on the market under his or her name or trademark or carries out a substantial modification of the product with digital elements already placed on the market.
