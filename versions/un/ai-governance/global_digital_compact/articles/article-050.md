50. At the same time, we must responsibly manage the potential risks posed by
science and technology, in particular the ways in which science, technology and
innovation can perpetuate and deepen divides, in particular the gender gap and
patterns of discrimination and inequality within and between countries and adversely
impact the enjoyment of human rights and progress on sustainable development. We
will deepen our partnerships with relevant stakeholders, especially the international
financial institutions, the private sector, the technical and academic communities and
civil society, and we will ensure that science, technology and innovation is a catalyst
