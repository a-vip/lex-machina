# Article 5 — A/RES/78/265 — Paragraph 5

5. Emphasizes that human rights and fundamental freedoms must be
respected, protected and promoted throughout the life cycle of artificial intelligence
systems, calls upon all Member States and, where applicable, other stakeholders to
refrain from or cease the use of artificial intelligence systems that are impossible to
operate in compliance with international human rights law or that pose undue risks to
the enjoyment of human rights, especially of those who are in vulnerable situations,
and reaffirms that the same rights that people have offline must also be protected
online, including throughout the life cycle of artificial intelligence systems;
Seizing the opportunities of safe, secure and trustworthy artificial
intelligence systems for sustainable development A/RES/78/265
