# Article 18 — Liability of legal persons

- **Instrument**: UN Cybercrime Convention (Hanoi)
- **Stage**: UNGA Adoption of UN Cybercrime Convention (A/RES/79/243) (`2024-12-24`)
- **Document Symbol**: `A/RES/79/243`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_18`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_pdf`
- **Institutional Author**: United Nations General Assembly `<unga@un.org>`

1. Each State Party shall adopt such measures as may be necessary, consistent with
its legal principles, to establish the liability of legal persons for participation in th e
offences established in accordance with this Convention.
2. Subject to the legal principles of the State Party, the liability of legal persons
may be criminal, civil or administrative.
3. Such liability shall be without prejudice to the criminal liability of the natural
persons who have committed the offences.
4. Each State Party shall, in particular, ensure that legal persons held liable in
accordance with this article are subject to effective, proportionate and dissuasive
criminal or non-criminal sanctions, including monetary sanctions.
