(70e)
The compliance with the transparency obligations for the AI systems coved by this
Regulation should not be interpreted as indicating th
at the use of the system or its output is
lawful under this Regulation or other Union and Member State law and should be without
prejudice to other transparency obligations for deployers of AI systems laid down in Union
or national law.
