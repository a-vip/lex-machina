- **Author**: EU Trilogue Negotiators `<trilogue@europa.eu>`

(60d)
When a general
-
purpose AI model is integrated into or forms part of an AI system, this
system should be considered a general
-
purpose AI system when, due to this integration,
this system has the capability to serve a variety
of purposes. A general
-
purpose AI system
can be used directly, or it may be integrated into other AI systems.
