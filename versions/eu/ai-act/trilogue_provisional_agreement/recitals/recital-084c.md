(84c)
Persons acting as ‘whistle
-
blowers’ on the breaches of this Regulation should be afforded
the protection guaranteed by Union legislation on the protection of
persons who report
breaches of law. Therefore, Directive (EU) 2019/1937 should apply to the reporting of
breaches of this Regulation and the protection of persons reporting such breaches.
