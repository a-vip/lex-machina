(60b)
Whereas the generality of a model could, among other criteria, also be
determined by a
number of parameters, models with at least a billion of parameters and trained with a large
amount of data using self
-
supervision at scale should be considered as displaying
significant generality and competently performing a wide range of
distinctive tasks.
