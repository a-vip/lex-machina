# Recital (67)

- **Instrument**: EU AI Act
- **Stage**: European Parliament Negotiating Mandate (`2023-06-14`)
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `rct_67`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_html`
- **Author**: European Parliament `<parliament@europa.eu>`

High-risk AI systems should bear the CE marking to indicate their conformity with this Regulation so that they can move freely within the internal market
. For physical high-risk AI systems, a physical CE marking should be affixed, and may be complemented by a digital CE marking. For digital only high-risk AI systems, a digital CE marking should be used
. Member States should not create unjustified obstacles to the placing on the market or putting into service of high-risk AI systems that comply with the requirements laid down in this Regulation and bear the CE marking.
