Member States and keep the list up to date.

-

technical means.
The market surveillance authority shall organise the testing with the close
involvement of the requesting public authority or body within reasonable time following
the request.

Any information and documentation obtained by the national public authoriti
es or bodies
referred to in paragraph 3 pursuant to the provisions of this Article shall be treated in
