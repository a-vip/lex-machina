(84)
Member States should take all necessary measures to
ensure that the provisions of this
Regulation are implemented, including by laying down effective, proportionate and
dissuasive penalties for their infringement, and in respect of
the
ne bis in idem
principle
. For
certain specific infringements, Member St
ates should take into account the margins and
criteria set out in this Regulation. The European Data Protection Supervisor should have the
power to impose fines on
Union institutions, agencies and bodies falling within the scope of
this Regulation.
