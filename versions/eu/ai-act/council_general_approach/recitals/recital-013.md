(13)
In order to ensure a consistent and high level of protection of public interests as regards
health, safety and fundament
al rights, common normative standards for all high
-
risk AI
systems should be established. Those standards should be consistent with the Charter of
fundamental rights of the European Union (the Charter) and should be non
-
discriminatory
and in line with the
Union’s international trade commitments.
