(56)
To enable enforcement of this Regulation and c
reate a level
-
playing field for operators, and
taking into account the different forms of making available of digital products, it is
important to ensure that, under all circumstances, a person established in the Union can
provide authorities with all the
necessary information on the compliance of an AI system.
Therefore, prior to making their AI systems available in the Union, where an importer
cannot be identified, providers established outside the Union shall, by written mandate,
appoint an authorised re
presentative established in the Union.
