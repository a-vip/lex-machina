# Article 26 — Notifying authorities

- **Instrument**: EU Cyber Resilience Act
- **Stage**: European Commission Proposal (`2022-09-15`)
- **Document Symbol**: `COM(2022) 454 final`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_26`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_xhtml_akn`
- **Institutional Author**: European Commission `<commission@europa.eu>`

1. Member States shall designate a notifying authority that shall be responsible for setting up and carrying out the necessary procedures for the assessment and notification of conformity assessment bodies and the monitoring of notified bodies, including compliance with Article 3 1 .
2. Member States may decide that the assessment and monitoring referred to in paragraph 1 shall be carried out by a national accreditation body within the meaning of and in accordance with Regulation (EC) No 765/2008.
