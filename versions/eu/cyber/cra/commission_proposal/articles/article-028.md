# Article 28 — Information obligation on notifying authorities

- **Instrument**: EU Cyber Resilience Act
- **Stage**: European Commission Proposal (`2022-09-15`)
- **Document Symbol**: `COM(2022) 454 final`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_28`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_xhtml_akn`
- **Institutional Author**: European Commission `<commission@europa.eu>`

1. Member States shall inform the Commiss ion of their procedures for the assessment and notification of conformity assessment bodies and the monitoring of notified bodies, and of any changes thereto.
2. The Commission shall make that information publicly available.
