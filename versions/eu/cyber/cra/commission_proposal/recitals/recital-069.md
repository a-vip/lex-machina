# Recital (69)

- **Instrument**: EU Cyber Resilience Act

(69) Economic operators should be provided with a sufficient time to adapt to the requirements of this Regulation. T his Regulation should apply [24 months] from its entry into force , with the exception of the reporting obligations concerning actively exploited vulnerabilities and incidents, which should apply [12 months] from the entry into force of this Regulation .
