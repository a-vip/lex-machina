# Article 52 — Unless he be a volunteer, no prisoner of war may be employed on labour which is of an unhealthy or dangerous nature.

- **Instrument**: Geneva Conventions I–III (1949)
- **Stage**: Geneva Convention (III) — Treatment of Prisoners of War (`1949-08-12`)
- **Document Symbol**: `75 UNTS 135 (GC III)`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_52`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_html`
- **Institutional Author**: Diplomatic Conference of Geneva `<ihl@icrc.org>`

No prisoner of war shall be assigned to labour which would be looked upon as humiliating for a member of the Detaining Power's own forces.
The removal of mines or similar devices shall be considered as dangerous labour.
