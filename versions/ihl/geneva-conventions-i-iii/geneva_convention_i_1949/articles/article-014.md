# Article 14

- **Instrument**: Geneva Conventions I–III (1949)
- **Stage**: Geneva Convention (I) — Wounded and Sick in Armed Forces in the Field (`1949-08-12`)
- **Document Symbol**: `75 UNTS 31 (GC I)`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_14`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_html`
- **Institutional Author**: Diplomatic Conference of Geneva `<ihl@icrc.org>`

Subject to the provisions of Article 12, the wounded and sick of a belligerent who fall into enemy hands shall be prisoners of war, and the provisions of international law concerning prisoners of war shall apply to them
