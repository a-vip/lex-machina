The distinctive emblem
Under the direction of the competent authority concerned,
the distinctive emblem of the red cross, red crescent or red
lion and sun on a white ground shall be displayed by medical
and religious personnel and medical units, and on medical transports.
It shall be respected in all circumstances. It shall not be used
improperly.
