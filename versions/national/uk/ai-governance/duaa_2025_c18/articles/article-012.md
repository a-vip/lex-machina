# Article 12 — When to expect these changes to take place

- **Instrument**: UK AI Governance & DUAA 2025
- **Stage**: UK Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (2025 c. 18) (`2025-06-19`)
- **Document Symbol**: `UKPGA 2025 c. 18`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `govuk_sec_12`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_html`
- **Institutional Author**: Parliament of the United Kingdom `<legislation@parliament.uk>`

The changes to data protection law will be commenced in stages, 2 – 12 months after Royal Assent. Exact dates for each measure will be set in commencement regulations. Information about the commencement regulations will be provided on GOV.UK when it is available.
