# Article 6 — 4. Scientific Research

- **Instrument**: UK AI Governance & DUAA 2025
- **Stage**: UK Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (2025 c. 18) (`2025-06-19`)
- **Document Symbol**: `UKPGA 2025 c. 18`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `govuk_sec_6`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_html`
- **Institutional Author**: Parliament of the United Kingdom `<legislation@parliament.uk>`

The DUAA clarifies that scientific research may include commercial research. It allows researchers to seek consent for broad areas of related research and clearly outlines the safeguards required for using personal data in research.
