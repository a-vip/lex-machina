# Article 2 — AI Strategy Headquarters, AI Basic Plan & Misuse Investigation Powers

- **Instrument**: Japan AI Promotion Act (2025)
- **Stage**: Japan AI Promotion Act (Act No. 53 of 2025 — Fully Effective Sept 1, 2025) (`2025-09-01`)
- **Document Symbol**: `JP Act No. 53 of 2025`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_2`
- **Provenance Mode**: `structured_regulatory_brief`
- **Institutional Author**: National Diet of Japan `<ai-strategy@cas.go.jp>`

Structured statutory record (effective Sept 1, 2025): Establishes the Prime Minister-led AI Strategy Headquarters and mandatory national AI Basic Plan, obligating private AI businesses to cooperate with government investigations into rights-infringing AI incidents and authorizing public naming/guidance for serious non-compliance.
