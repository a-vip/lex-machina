# Article 1 — Enactment & Innovation-First Architecture — Japan AI Promotion Act (Act No. 53 of 2025)

- **Instrument**: Japan AI Promotion Act (2025)
- **Stage**: Japan AI Promotion Act (Act No. 53 of 2025 — Fully Effective Sept 1, 2025) (`2025-09-01`)
- **Document Symbol**: `JP Act No. 53 of 2025`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_1`
- **Provenance Mode**: `structured_regulatory_brief`
- **Institutional Author**: National Diet of Japan `<ai-strategy@cas.go.jp>`

Structured statutory record (enacted May 28, 2025; promulgated June 4, 2025; fully in force Sept 1, 2025): Establishes Japan's statutory 'Act on Promotion of Research, Development, and Utilization of Artificial Intelligence-Related Technologies' prioritizing R&D acceleration, compute/data infrastructure, and international regulatory interoperability without EU-style upfront administrative fines.
