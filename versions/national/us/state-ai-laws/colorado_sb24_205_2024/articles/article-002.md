Structured statutory record (2024-2026): AB 2013 (effective Jan 1, 2026) mandates high-level generative AI training dataset transparency disclosures; SB 53 (TFAIA) targets frontier foundation models exceeding 10^26 FLOPs with public catastrophic risk frameworks, critical safety incident reporting to California OES, and frontier lab whistleblower protections.
