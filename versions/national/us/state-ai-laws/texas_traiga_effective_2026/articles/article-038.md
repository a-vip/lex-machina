Sec.
554.103.
LIMITATION OF AUTHORITY. The council may
not:
(1) adopt rules or promulgate guidance that is binding
for any entity;
(2) interfere with or override the operation of a
state agency; or
(3)
perform a duty or exercise a power not granted by
this chapter.
