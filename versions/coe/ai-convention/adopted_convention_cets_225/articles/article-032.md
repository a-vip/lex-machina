# Article 32 — Territorial application

- **Instrument**: CoE AI Convention (CETS 225)
- **Stage**: Adopted Framework Convention (CETS 225) + Explanatory Report (`2024-05-17`)
- **Document Symbol**: `CETS No. 225`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_32`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_pdf`
- **Institutional Author**: Council of Europe Committee of Ministers `<cm@coe.int>`

1 Any State or the European Union may, at the time of signature or when depositing its instrument
of ratification, acceptance, approval or accession, specify the territory or territories to which this
Convention shall apply.
2 Any Party may, at a later date, by a declaration addressed to the Secretary General of the
Council of Europe, extend the application of this Convention to any other territory specified in
the declaration. In respect of such territory, this Convention shall enter into force on the first
day of the month following the expiration of a period of three months after the date of receipt of
the declaration by the Secretary General.
