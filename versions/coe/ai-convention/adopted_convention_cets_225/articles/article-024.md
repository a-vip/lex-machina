# Article 24 — Reporting obligation

- **Instrument**: CoE AI Convention (CETS 225)
- **Stage**: Adopted Framework Convention (CETS 225) + Explanatory Report (`2024-05-17`)
- **Document Symbol**: `CETS No. 225`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_24`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_pdf`
- **Institutional Author**: Council of Europe Committee of Ministers `<cm@coe.int>`

1 Each Party shall provide a report to the Conference of the Parties within the first two years after
becoming a Party, and then periodically thereafter with details of the activities undertaken to
give effect to Article 3, paragraph 1, sub-paragraphs a and b.
2 The Conference of the Parties shall determine the format and the process for the report in
accordance with its rules of procedure.
