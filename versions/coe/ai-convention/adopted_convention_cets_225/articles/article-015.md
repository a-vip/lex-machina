# Article 15 — Procedural safeguards

- **Instrument**: CoE AI Convention (CETS 225)
- **Stage**: Adopted Framework Convention (CETS 225) + Explanatory Report (`2024-05-17`)
- **Document Symbol**: `CETS No. 225`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_15`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_pdf`
- **Institutional Author**: Council of Europe Committee of Ministers `<cm@coe.int>`

1 Each Party shall ensure that, where an artificial intelligence system significantly impacts upon
the enjoyment of human rights, effective procedural guarantees, safeguards and rights, in
accordance with the applicable international and domestic law, are available to persons affected
thereby.
