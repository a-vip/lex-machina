# Article 6 — Requirements regarding respect for human rights

- **Instrument**: CoE AI Convention (CETS 225)
- **Stage**: CAI Revised Zero Draft (`2023-01-06`)
- **Document Symbol**: `CAI(2023)01`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_6`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_pdf`
- **Institutional Author**: Committee on Artificial Intelligence (CAI) `<cai@coe.int>`

Each Party shall take such measures as are aimed at minimising and, to the extent possible,
preventing any unlawful harm or damage to human rights and fundamental freedoms, which could
result from the inappropriate application of artificial intelligence systems by public authorities.
