# Article 7 — Illegal access

- **Instrument**: UN Cybercrime Convention (Hanoi)
- **Stage**: UNGA Adoption of UN Cybercrime Convention (A/RES/79/243) (`2024-12-24`)
- **Document Symbol**: `A/RES/79/243`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_7`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_pdf`
- **Institutional Author**: United Nations General Assembly `<unga@un.org>`

1. Each State Party shall adopt such legislative and other measures as may be
necessary to establish as a criminal offence under its domestic law, when committed
intentionally, the access to the whole or any part of an information and
communications technology system without right.
2. A State Party may require that the offence be committed by infringing security
measures, with the intent of obtaining electronic data or other dishonest or criminal
intent or in relation to an information and communications technology system that is
connected to another information and communications technology system.
