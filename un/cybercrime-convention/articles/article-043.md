# Article 43 — International cooperation for the purpose of expedited disclosure of preserved

- **Instrument**: UN Cybercrime Convention (Hanoi)
- **Stage**: UNGA Adoption of UN Cybercrime Convention (A/RES/79/243) (`2024-12-24`)
- **Document Symbol**: `A/RES/79/243`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_43`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_pdf`
- **Institutional Author**: United Nations General Assembly `<unga@un.org>`

traffic data
1. Where, in the course of the execution of a request made pursuant to article 42
of this Convention to preserve traffic data concerning a specific communication, the
requested State Party discovers that a service provider in another State Party was
involved in the transmission of the communication, the requested State Party shall
expeditiously disclose to the requesting State Party a sufficient amount of traffic data
to identify that service provider and the path through which the communication was
transmitted.
2. Disclosure of traffic data under paragraph 1 of this article may be refused only
on the basis of the grounds contained in article 40, paragraph 21 (b) and (c) and
paragraph 22, of this Convention.
