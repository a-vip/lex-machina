# Article 7 — Principle (g) — Risk Assessment and Mitigation During Design

- **Instrument**: UN CCW GGE on LAWS
- **Stage**: CCW GGE 11 Guiding Principles on LAWS (`2019-08-21`)
- **Document Symbol**: `CCW/GGE.1/2019/3`
- **Institutional Author**: UN CCW GGE `<ccw@un.org>`

Risk assessments and mitigation measures should be part of the design, development, testing and deployment cycle of emerging technologies in any weapons systems.
