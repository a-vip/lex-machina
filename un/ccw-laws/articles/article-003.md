# Article 3 — Second Tier — Regulated Autonomous Weapons Systems (Paragraph 22)

- **Instrument**: UN CCW GGE on LAWS
- **Stage**: CCW GGE Report & Rolling Text Evolution (`2023-05-26`)
- **Document Symbol**: `CCW/GGE.1/2023/2`
- **Institutional Author**: UN CCW GGE `<ccw@un.org>`

1. For other weapons systems based on emerging technologies in the area of LAWS that select and apply force to targets without human intervention after activation, States must implement rigorous regulatory measures to ensure compliance with IHL, including:
(a) limiting the types of targets that the system can engage;
(b) limiting the duration, geographical scope, and scale of operation, including through self-destruct, self-deactivation, or self-neutralization mechanisms;
(c) ensuring clear human-machine interaction, understandability, and predictability of system behavior in the specific operational environment; and
(d) enabling human operators to supervise, intervene, and deactivate the system where appropriate.
