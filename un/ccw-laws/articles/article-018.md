# Article 18 — CCW/GGE.1/2023/2 — Paragraph 18

18. The Group recalled that the right of parties to an armed conflict to choose methods or
means of warfare is not unlimited. Furthermore, international law, in particular the United
Nations Charter and International Humanitarian Law (IHL) as well as relevant ethical
perspectives, should continue to guide the work of the Group. The Group reaffirmed the
relevant paragraphs related to emerging technologies in the area of LAWS of documents
CCW/CONF.VI/11 and CCW/CONF.V/10.
