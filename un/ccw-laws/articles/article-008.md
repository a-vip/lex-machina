# Article 8 — Principle (h) — Anthropomorphism Avoidance

- **Instrument**: UN CCW GGE on LAWS
- **Stage**: CCW GGE 11 Guiding Principles on LAWS (`2019-08-21`)
- **Document Symbol**: `CCW/GGE.1/2019/3`
- **Institutional Author**: UN CCW GGE `<ccw@un.org>`

Consideration should be given to the use of emerging technologies in the area of lethal autonomous weapons systems in upholding compliance with IHL and other applicable international legal obligations; in policy discussions, anthropomorphising emerging technologies should be avoided.
