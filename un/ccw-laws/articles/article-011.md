# Article 11 — Principle (k) — Cyber Security and Data Integrity

- **Instrument**: UN CCW GGE on LAWS
- **Stage**: CCW GGE 11 Guiding Principles on LAWS (`2019-08-21`)
- **Document Symbol**: `CCW/GGE.1/2019/3`
- **Institutional Author**: UN CCW GGE `<ccw@un.org>`

Discussions and any potential policy measures taken within the context of the CCW should not hamper progress in or access to civilian research and development and use of autonomous technologies, while ensuring resilience against cyber interference and spoofing.
