# Article 5 — National Legal Weapons Reviews and Lifecycle Testing (Paragraphs 24–26)

- **Instrument**: UN CCW GGE on LAWS
- **Stage**: CCW GGE Report & Rolling Text Evolution (`2023-05-26`)
- **Document Symbol**: `CCW/GGE.1/2023/2`
- **Institutional Author**: UN CCW GGE `<ccw@un.org>`

1. National legal reviews of new weapons, means or methods of warfare, including those based on emerging technologies in the area of LAWS, are a critical measure for States to ensure compliance with IHL.
2. Reviews should be conducted at multiple stages of the lifecycle, including study, development, acquisition, and whenever significant modifications (such as machine learning retraining) alter the operational capabilities or effects of the weapon system.
