# Article 9 — Principle (i) — Preservation of Peaceful Dual-Use AI Innovation

- **Instrument**: UN CCW GGE on LAWS
- **Stage**: CCW GGE 11 Guiding Principles on LAWS (`2019-08-21`)
- **Document Symbol**: `CCW/GGE.1/2019/3`
- **Institutional Author**: UN CCW GGE `<ccw@un.org>`

In crafting potential policy measures, emerging technologies in the area of lethal autonomous weapons systems should not be anthropomorphized and policy measures should not hamper progress in or access to peaceful uses of intelligent autonomous technologies.
