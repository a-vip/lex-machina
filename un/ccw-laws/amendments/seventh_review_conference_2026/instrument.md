## Enacting Terms (Articles)

### Article 1 — 2024-2026 GGE Operational Characteristics & CCW Seventh Review Conference Outcome

Analytical milestone record (2026): Concludes the 2024-2026 GGE mandate defining LAWS operational characteristics (systems identifying, selecting, and engaging targets resulting in loss of life without human intervention). At the CCW Seventh Review Conference, major-power divisions deferred a legally binding treaty negotiation mandate to a subsequent multi-year cycle.
