# Article 2 — Common Article 2 — Application in International Armed Conflicts

- **Instrument**: Geneva Conventions & Additional Protocols
- **Stage**: Additional Protocols I and II (1977) (`1977-06-08`)
- **Document Symbol**: `1125 UNTS 3 / 1125 UNTS 609`
- **Institutional Author**: Diplomatic Conference on the Reaffirmation and Development of IHL (CDDH) `<ihl@icrc.org>`

1. In addition to the provisions which shall be implemented in peacetime, the Conventions and Protocol Additional I apply to all cases of declared war or of any other armed conflict which may arise between two or more High Contracting Parties, as well as armed conflicts in which peoples are fighting against colonial domination and alien occupation and against racist regimes in the exercise of their right of self-determination.
