# Article 3 — Common Article 3 & Additional Protocol II — Non-International Armed Conflicts

- **Instrument**: Geneva Conventions & Additional Protocols
- **Stage**: Additional Protocols I and II (1977) (`1977-06-08`)
- **Document Symbol**: `1125 UNTS 3 / 1125 UNTS 609`
- **Institutional Author**: Diplomatic Conference on the Reaffirmation and Development of IHL (CDDH) `<ihl@icrc.org>`

1. Protocol Additional II develops and supplements Article 3 common to the Geneva Conventions of 12 August 1949 without modifying its existing conditions of application.
2. Neither the civilian population as such, nor individual civilians, shall be the object of attack. Acts or threats of violence the primary purpose of which is to spread terror among the civilian population are prohibited (AP II Article 13).
