# Article 15

- **Instrument**: Geneva Conventions I–III (1949)
- **Stage**: Geneva Convention (II) — Wounded, Sick and Shipwrecked at Sea (`1949-08-12`)
- **Document Symbol**: `75 UNTS 85 (GC II)`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_15`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_html`
- **Institutional Author**: Diplomatic Conference of Geneva `<ihl@icrc.org>`

If wounded, sick or shipwrecked persons are taken on board a neutral warship or a neutral military aircraft, it shall be ensured, where so required by international law, that they can take no further part in operations of war.
