# Article 23

- **Instrument**: Geneva Conventions I–III (1949)
- **Stage**: Geneva Convention (II) — Wounded, Sick and Shipwrecked at Sea (`1949-08-12`)
- **Document Symbol**: `75 UNTS 85 (GC II)`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_23`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_html`
- **Institutional Author**: Diplomatic Conference of Geneva `<ihl@icrc.org>`

Establishments ashore entitled to the protection of the Geneva Convention for the Amelioration of the Condition of the Wounded and Sick in Armed Forces in the Field of August 12, 1949 shall be protected from bombardment or attack from the sea.
