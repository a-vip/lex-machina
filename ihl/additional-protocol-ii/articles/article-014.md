# Article 14 — Protection of objects indispensable to the

- **Instrument**: Additional Protocol II (1977)
- **Stage**: Additional Protocol II (1977) — NIAC Protection of Victims (`1977-06-08`)
- **Document Symbol**: `1125 UNTS 609 (AP II)`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_14`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_html`
- **Institutional Author**: Diplomatic Conference on the Reaffirmation and Development of IHL (CDDH) `<ihl@icrc.org>`

survival of the civilian population
Starvation of civilians as a method of combat is prohibited.
It is therefore prohibited to attack, destroy, remove or render
useless, for that purpose, objects indispensable to the survival
of the civilian population, such as foodstuffs, agricultural
areas for the production of foodstuffs, crops, livestock, drinking
water installations and supplies and irrigation works.
