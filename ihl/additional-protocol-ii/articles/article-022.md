# Article 22 — Accession

- **Instrument**: Additional Protocol II (1977)
- **Stage**: Additional Protocol II (1977) — NIAC Protection of Victims (`1977-06-08`)

This Protocol shall be open for accession by any Party to
the Conventions which has not signed it. The instruments of accession
shall be deposited with the depositary.
