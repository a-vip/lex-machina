# Article 18 — Relief societies and relief actions

- **Instrument**: Additional Protocol II (1977)
- **Stage**: Additional Protocol II (1977) — NIAC Protection of Victims (`1977-06-08`)
- **Document Symbol**: `1125 UNTS 609 (AP II)`
- **Akoma Ntoso eId**: `art_18`
- **Provenance Mode**: `full_primary_html`
- **Institutional Author**: Diplomatic Conference on the Reaffirmation and Development of IHL (CDDH) `<ihl@icrc.org>`

1. Relief societies located in the territory of the High Contracting
Party, such as Red Cross (Red Crescent, Red Lion and Sun) organizations,
may offer their services for the performance of their traditional
functions in relation to the victims of the armed conflict. The
civilian population may, even on its own initiative, offer to
collect and care for the wounded, sick and shipwrecked.
2. If the civilian population is suffering undue hardship
owing to a lack of the supplies essential for its survival, such
as foodstuffs and medical supplies, relief actions for the civilian
population which are of an exclusively humanitarian and impartial
nature and which are conducted without any adverse distinction
shall be undertaken subject to the consent of the High Contracting
Party concerned.
